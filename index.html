ABOUT

Dan Golden has spent his life chasing the clock - and now he wants to help other runners do the same. A Texas 6A Individual and Team State Champion in cross country, Dan went on to run cross country and track at The University of Texas, competing at the Division 1 level against the best in the country.

Throughout his career, Dan has trained under several different coaches and experienced just as many different training methodologies. He still leans on those same coaches and what he learned from them to guide his own training. That experience is why he brings a unique edge to his coaching and can provide tailored guidance based on the athlete's specific needs.

Dan coaches the mental side of the sport as seriously as the physical: discipline to put in the work nobody sees, real enjoyment of the process, and a genuine belief in yourself when a race gets hard.

His athletes consistently point to the same thing: he shows up for them. Motivating people is one of Dan's biggest strengths, and it shows in how he coaches — direct, invested, and genuinely committed to seeing every runner he works with achieve their goal.

Questions

Do I need to be an experienced racer?

No. The program is built for any runner chasing a PR, from your first 5K to your fifth marathon.

Is coaching only available in Austin?

In-person coaching is available in Austin, TX, and full remote coaching is available anywhere for every plan level.

How does the custom training plan work?

After you submit your interest, Dan will contact you to learn more about your current mileage, race goals, and schedule. Then, he'll build your custom plan and adjust it week-to-week based on your check-ins.

Can I change plans later?

Yes — you can move between Base and Build at any time as your training needs change.
