ABOUT
[ Photo placeholder — Dan Golden, racing or coaching shot ]

Dan Golden has spent his life chasing the clock — and helping other runners chase theirs. A Texas 6A state champion in both the individual and team cross country races, Dan went on to run cross country and track at the University of Texas, competing at the collegiate level against the best in the country.

Along the way, Dan trained under half a dozen different coaches — high school, club, and Division I — and saw just as many approaches to building a runner, from old-school mileage to modern polarized training. That range is why he doesn't coach from a single formula. He coaches the athlete in front of him.

Dan coaches the mental side of the sport as seriously as the physical: discipline to put in the work nobody sees, real enjoyment of the process, and a genuine belief in yourself when a race gets hard.

His athletes consistently point to the same thing: he shows up for them. Motivating people is one of Dan's biggest strengths, and it shows in how he coaches — direct, invested, and genuinely committed to seeing every runner he works with hit their best outcome.

Questions

Do I need to be an experienced racer?

No. The program is built for any runner chasing a PR, from your first 5K to your fifth marathon.

Is coaching only available in Austin?

In-person coaching is available in Austin, TX, and full remote coaching is available anywhere for every plan level.

How does the custom training plan work?

After your application, Dan builds a plan based on your current mileage, race goals, and schedule, then adjusts it week to week based on your check-ins.

Can I change plans later?

Yes — you can move between Base, Build, and Peak at any time as your training needs change.
